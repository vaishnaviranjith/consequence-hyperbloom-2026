Why teams use it

Operational changes rarely affect only the thing being changed. CONSEQUENCE helps decision-makers rehearse the cascade before committing time, money, capacity, or people.

01 · SEE THE CASCADE

Trace downstream impact

Move from a proposed change to the dependencies that may be affected, with an inspectable propagation path.

02 · PRIORITIZE RISK

Focus on what matters

Rank consequences by deterministic impact scoring instead of presenting an unstructured list of AI-generated warnings.

03 · ACT BEFORE ROLLOUT

Get required actions

Translate technical graph impacts into operational consequences, mitigation steps, and an executive recommendation.
